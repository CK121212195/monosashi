The Four-Day Workweek Experiment

社会・ビジネス 英検2級〜準1級レベル（TOEIC 600〜750相当） 202語・設問3問

The idea of working four days a week for the same pay once sounded like a fantasy. In the past few years, however, a growing number of companies in countries including the United Kingdom, Iceland, and Japan have experimented with it, and the results have attracted considerable attention.

In many trials, productivity held steady or even improved, while employees reported less stress and burnout. Companies found that when time was limited, staff cut unnecessary meetings and focused on essential tasks. Several also said the policy helped them attract and retain talented workers in a competitive labor market.

Skeptics caution that the model does not suit every industry. Hospitals, factories, and shops that must be staffed every day cannot simply close for an extra day, and compressing the same workload into fewer days can increase fatigue rather than reduce it. Participants in trials were also volunteers, which may have made the results look better than they would be across the economy.

Rather than a single universal solution, the four-day week may be best seen as one option among many flexible arrangements. Its broader lesson may be that the number of hours worked is not always the best measure of how much gets done.

設問（3問）

Q1 According to the trials, how did companies maintain productivity?

  1. By hiring more part-time workers.
  2. Staff eliminated unnecessary meetings.
  3. By reducing employees' pay.
  4. By extending working hours on other days.
正解と解説を見る

正解：2. Staff eliminated unnecessary meetings.

設問訳：試行によると、企業はどのように生産性を維持したか。

staff cut unnecessary meetings and focused on essential tasks が根拠。3は for the same pay（同じ給料で）と矛盾します。

根拠：第2段落「staff cut unnecessary meetings」

Q2 Why might the trial results be overly positive?

  1. The companies that took part chose to do so voluntarily.
  2. The trials lasted for only one day.
  3. Employees were paid extra to report good results.
  4. Only hospitals took part in the trials.
正解と解説を見る

正解：1. The companies that took part chose to do so voluntarily.

設問訳：試行の結果が実際よりよく見えている可能性があるのはなぜか。

Participants in trials were also volunteers。自ら参加した企業は制度に向いている可能性が高い、という「選択バイアス」の指摘です。統計的な読み方が問われる良問パターン。

根拠：第3段落「Participants in trials were also volunteers」

Q3 What is the author's overall view?

  1. Every company should adopt a four-day week immediately.
  2. The four-day week has completely failed.
  3. It is one useful option, not a solution for everyone.
  4. Working hours are the best measure of productivity.
正解と解説を見る

正解：3. It is one useful option, not a solution for everyone.

設問訳：筆者の全体的な見解はどれか。

Rather than a single universal solution ... one option among many flexible arrangements が結論。4は最終文の内容と正反対です。

根拠：第4段落「one option among many flexible arrangements」

全訳を見る

第1段落　同じ給料で週4日働くという考えは、かつては夢物語のように聞こえた。しかしこの数年、英国、アイスランド、日本などの国々で、それを試す企業が増えており、その結果はかなりの注目を集めている。

第2段落　多くの試行で、生産性は維持されるか、むしろ向上し、従業員はストレスや燃え尽きが減ったと報告した。企業は、時間が限られると従業員が不要な会議を減らし、本質的な業務に集中することに気づいた。人材獲得競争の激しい労働市場で、この制度が優秀な人材を引きつけ、引き留めるのに役立ったと述べる企業もいくつかあった。

第3段落　懐疑的な人々は、このモデルがすべての業界に合うわけではないと注意を促す。毎日人員を配置しなければならない病院や工場、店舗は、単純にもう1日休むわけにはいかないし、同じ仕事量をより少ない日数に詰め込めば、疲労が減るどころか増えることもある。また、試行の参加者は自ら手を挙げた企業だったため、経済全体で実施した場合よりも結果がよく見えた可能性がある。

第4段落　週休3日制は、万人向けの唯一の解決策というより、数ある柔軟な働き方の選択肢の1つと見るのがよいのかもしれない。そこから得られるより広い教訓は、働いた時間数が、どれだけの仕事が成し遂げられたかを測る最良の物差しとは限らない、ということなのかもしれない。
